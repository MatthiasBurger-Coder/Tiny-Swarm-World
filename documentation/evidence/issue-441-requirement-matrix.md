Classification: credential/source restrictions are security constraints; resume,
partial results, backup and migration rules are resilience constraints; preview
and actionable errors are UX requirements; identity/drift/stage evidence is
observability; resource/host bounds are non-functional. Historical compatibility
is an assumption verified against source. New profiles and removal apply are
explicit non-goals, not unresolved W01 requirements. Downstream admission needs
concrete qualified transitions; absence of those results is not W01 live success.

The phrase COVERED means the W01 inventory/contract/scenario obligation has
